About me

I'm a backend software engineer in Seattle with more than ten years of experience building the systems behind large-scale commerce, advertising and payments products.

Most recently I was a Staff Software Engineer on Coupang's Price Determination team, which is responsible for how prices are set across the marketplace. Before that I spent over six years at Facebook, working on teams in ads delivery, billing and revenue infrastructure, and privacy-focused analytics. Earlier in my career I worked on e-commerce at Gear Launch and international payments at Xoom, a PayPal service.

Across these teams, my work has centered on keeping critical backend services reliable, secure and scalable as they grow. I studied Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at UC Berkeley.

Inside a Neural Network is a side project, built with Claude Code: Claude wrote the code, and I directed the design. It's a neural network written from scratch, with no machine-learning libraries, so that every step of learning can be seen and played with. I first learned to build a neural network by following Michael Nielsen's free online book, Neural Networks and Deep Learning.
